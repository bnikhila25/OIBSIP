Industrialist. Philanthropist. The man who proved that business and humanity need not walk separate paths.

Who is Ratan Tata?

Ratan Tata is one of India's most respected business leaders and philanthropists. As the former chairman of the Tata Group, he transformed the organization into a global enterprise while maintaining strong ethical values. His vision, humility, and commitment to society have inspired millions around the world.

📅 Born: December 28, 1937
🎓 Education: Cornell University
🏢 Former Chairman of Tata Group
🏅 Padma Bhushan & Padma Vibhushan
